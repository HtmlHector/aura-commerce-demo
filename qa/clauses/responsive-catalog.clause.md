# Invariant
Product cards display accurate pricing, title, and interactive Add to Cart actions across all viewport sizes.

## Behavioral Rules
- On desktop (1280x800), product grid renders in 4 columns.
- On mobile (390x844), product grid adapts cleanly to 1 column.
- Clicking 'Add to Cart' opens the sliding cart drawer immediately.
